# Night of 12.09.2026 - the bank filter was a look-ahead, and is gone

Continues reports/NIGHT_2026_09_11.md.

## The headline: everything about the filter is withdrawn

The filter (commit 9720ae4, wired in b59c03a, both reverted) attached to each signal a row of 140
structure/indicator features. The join was wrong:

* `pullback_bank.hourly_signals` stamps a signal with the hour **close**: `close = bar_start + 3600`.
* the research feature pack (`big/SYM.npz`, built from `mrlib.bars`) stamps each row with the bar
  **start**.
* joining them on equality handed the model the hour that was only then **beginning** - the entry
  bar and the 45 minutes after it.

Caught by running the live modules against the research on truncated history: 58 of 140 features
disagreed, and the bot's score for hour X equalled the research score for hour X-1. The bot was
right (`bank_filter.features` reads the last CLOSED hour); the research leaked.

Re-measured with the correct join, fit 2022-23, cut chosen on 2024, exam read once:

| generator | selection skill (exam) | unfiltered | filtered |
|---|---|---|---|
| bank9, gate off | **0.483** (below chance) | +1.7R/mo, DD -21.3R, ratio 0.08 | +1.0R/mo, DD -22.8R, ratio 0.04 |
| strict3+short2_wide, gate on | **0.514** | +3.5R/mo, DD -11.1R, ratio 0.31 | +2.6R/mo, DD -9.7R, ratio 0.27 |

With the leaking join the same models scored 0.786 and 0.630. All of that skill was reading the
future hour.

**Withdrawn:** 89.2% / +4.0R/month / ratio 0.80 (11.09), and everything measured tonight on top of
it - 97.6% / +7.6R/month / 30 positive months out of 30, the "bank9 is the right generator"
conclusion, and every comparison that ran through the filter (K=2 and K=3 positions per coin,
28 coins, caps on concurrency, farther takes, mixing with the 4h trend, size-by-score). Those
experiments are not evidence any more; they were run against a model that could see ahead.

Nothing reached production: the bank and the filter were both off, nothing was pushed, and the
owner never gave the go-ahead. b59c03a has been reverted - the model file, the feature modules,
the wiring and its tests are out of the tree, so a future "yes" cannot switch on junk. 128 tests
pass.

## What still stands

* **The bank itself**, unchanged and audit-clean: 2.0 trades/day, 84.7% win rate, +0.056R per
  trade, +3.5R/month, drawdown -11.1R on 2025-26. Still off (`PULLBACK_LIVE_ENABLED=0`).
* **Ties do not exist in this book.** Of 1113 exam trades, 378 eventually touched both levels and
  **0** touched them inside the same 15m bar. The take sits 0.5-1.0 ATR away and the stop 3-4 ATR,
  so no result depends on intrabar ordering. Measured on the (leaking) filtered subset, but the
  measurement itself uses only prices and holds for the book.
* **HistGradientBoosting is deterministic here**: five seeds gave bit-identical predictions, so
  "robust across seeds" never tested anything and is withdrawn as evidence.
* **The rolling-cut warm-up trap** (method, not a result): an adaptive cut warmed up on the very
  window it was judged on; with a 400-signal window only three months of 2024 survived and a
  drawdown-free three-month sample won the selection. Equalise warm-up before comparing adaptive
  knobs.

## Lesson recorded

The 11.09 audit checked that the live feature factory reproduces the research factory on truncated
history - and it passed, 140/140. It could not catch this, because the bug was not in the factory
but in **which row was attached to which trade**. Feature-level parity is not alignment. From now
on the audit compares the live module's answer for a specific signal with the research row used
for that same signal, not just the factory output.
